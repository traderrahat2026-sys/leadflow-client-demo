Welcome to the LeadFlow Demo

LeadFlow is a CRM concept for HVAC businesses. Use the navigation to explore the workflow with sample customer records.

  1. Leads: Add, edit, search, filter and manage enquiries.
  2. Customers: Review customer contact details and activity history.
  3. Follow-ups: Organize actions by overdue, today and upcoming priority rules.
  4. Appointments: Schedule sample inspections, repairs and service visits.
  5. Quotes: Create and edit estimates, change quote status and export quote data.
  6. Reports: Review lead conversion, pipeline and accepted quote value.
Demo limitation: This preview stores information in this browser only. It does not send real emails or SMS, take payments, sync between users or provide secure cloud accounts. Those features can be built in a production version after requirements are agreed.
